Add one flag to an existing ArgumentParser. The library walks its subparsers directly, including nested commands.

KNOW THE BOUNDARY

Parser access gives the clearest result.

External inspection is useful, but CLI help text has no standard machine-readable format. Discovery may miss commands, and slow-starting applications can take time to inspect. For your own application, integrate with argparse directly.
